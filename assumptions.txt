1. PostgreSQL for relational data; SQL migrations for schema changes.

2. Customer and product data come from external APIs. Store product
   snapshots at purchase time for historical accuracy.

3. Store money as integer cents to avoid floating-point errors.

4. Maintain current balances and an append-only credit ledger
   recording every credit movement with reason and timestamp.

5. Support full and partial refunds without exceeding the
   original purchase amount.

6. Handle shipment failures through purchase statuses and
   compensating credit entries.

7. Core tables:
   credit_accounts, credit_ledger, purchases,
   purchase_items, refunds.

8. Reserve credit when an order is placed. Capture the reserved
   amount after successful shipment, or release it if shipment
   fails. Track in the credit ledger for full auditability.
